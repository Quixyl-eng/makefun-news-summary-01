Wednesday 11 November 2026. US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended with Russia and Ukraine agreeing in principle to a large prisoner exchange, not a peace deal, Al Jazeera reported. Rustem Umerov and Vladimir Medinsky confirmed it. Jared Kushner said the numbers would be set Thursday, then he and Steve Witkoff would meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Volodymyr Zelensky in Dublin. TASS, citing Gen. Alexander Zorin, claimed more than 1,000 a side; neither capital confirmed that, the Kyiv Independent said. Reuters said territory remained unresolved. Marco Rubio called the talks very productive but far from solved, Anadolu reported.

The Kyiv Independent said Russian strikes hit six substations in Vinnytsia, Kirovohrad, Zhytomyr and Cherkasy, and a market strike in Chornomorsk killed at least four and injured eight, Governor Oleh Kiper said. Ukraine's General Staff said drones damaged five oil tanks, 150,000 cubic metres, at Ust-Luga.

Reuters reported another Strait of Hormuz tanker incident, logged by UK Maritime Trade Operations, and said Iran reported a tanker ablaze after hitting a mine. At COP31 in Antalya, Turkey asked states to raise electricity's share of energy use to 35% by 2035, Anadolu reported, and Masoud Pezeshkian asked for compensation for damage from US and Israeli strikes, Reuters reported.

Reuters said Senator Kevin Cramer told Politico that Nvidia chief Jensen Huang should resign over an alleged tie to a Chinese businesswoman described as an alleged spy, after about $430 billion of market value was lost in two sessions. An aide said Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka resigned after courts rejected his education papers, Reuters reported. Unions said about four million Spanish workers held the first general strike in 14 years, over housing, Reuters reported. Pope Leo arrived in Peru; Tourism Minister Rogers Valencia expected some 850,000 domestic and 50,000 foreign visitors, Reuters reported.
