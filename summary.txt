Wednesday 11 November 2026. Russia and Ukraine agreed in principle to a prisoner exchange, not a peace deal, in Abu Dhabi, the Associated Press reported. Jared Kushner said the numbers would be set Thursday, before he and Steve Witkoff would meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Volodymyr Zelensky in Dublin. Reuters said territory was unresolved.

Russian attacks killed nine and injured 39, and two missiles entered Moldova and returned, the Kyiv Independent reported. An Odesa-region market strike killed three and injured 11, and a fire at Russia's Ust-Luga port injured three, the Associated Press reported.

Mohsen Rezaei said the Strait of Hormuz stays shut until the US meets seven conditions, including sanctions and the naval blockade, Al Jazeera reported. UK Maritime Trade Operations logged another tanker incident, and Iran said a tanker caught fire after a mine, Reuters reported.

At COP31 in Antalya, Turkey proposed raising electricity to 35% of energy use by 2035, from a little over 20%, Reuters reported. Masoud Pezeshkian sought compensation for damage from US and Israeli strikes, Reuters reported.
Nvidia lost about $430 billion in two sessions, and Senator Kevin Cramer told Politico late Tuesday that Jensen Huang should resign over an alleged affair with a woman described as a Chinese spy, Reuters reported. Four million Spanish workers held the first general strike in 14 years, over housing, with clashes in Madrid and Barcelona, Reuters reported.

Saudi forces intercepted Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Mecca, Reuters reported.

Storm Charles cancelled more than 200 flights in Portugal and closed schools; Mario Silvestre said 538 incidents were logged, Reuters reported. AFP reported that flight data showed at least a dozen US aircraft had left RAF Mildenhall, after a Wall Street Journal report of an Iranian threat. Pope Leo arrived in Peru; Tourism Minister Rogers Valencia expected 850,000 domestic and 50,000 foreign visitors, Reuters reported.
