Wednesday 11 November 2026. Russia and Ukraine agreed to a prisoner exchange, not a peace deal, as talks in Abu Dhabi ended, the Associated Press reported. Vladimir Medinsky said the agreement was in principle, and he and Rustem Umerov confirmed it. Jared Kushner said the numbers would be set Thursday, when he and Steve Witkoff meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Dublin. Associated Press said territory and security guarantees stayed open.

Russian attacks killed nine and injured 39, and two missiles entered Moldova and returned, the Kyiv Independent said. Oleh Kiper said an Odesa market strike killed three and injured 11, the Associated Press reported. Alexander Drozdenko said drones set the Ust-Luga port on fire, injuring three, and that Russia downed 627 drones, 150 near St. Petersburg.

Mohsen Rezaei said the Strait of Hormuz stays shut until the US meets seven conditions, including sanctions and the naval blockade, Al Jazeera reported. UK Maritime Trade Operations logged a tanker incident, and Iran said a tanker caught fire after a mine, Reuters reported.

At COP31 in Antalya, Turkey proposed electricity at 35% of energy use by 2035, from a little over 20%, Reuters reported. Masoud Pezeshkian sought compensation for damage from US and Israeli strikes, Reuters reported.

Nvidia lost about $430 billion in two sessions, and Senator Kevin Cramer told Politico late Tuesday that Jensen Huang should resign over an alleged affair with a woman described as a Chinese spy, Reuters reported. Four million Spanish workers held the first general strike in 14 years over housing, with clashes in Madrid and Barcelona, Reuters reported.

Saudi forces intercepted Houthi missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh and Mecca, Reuters reported. Storm Charles cancelled more than 200 flights in Portugal; Mario Silvestre logged 538 incidents, Reuters reported. AFP said at least a dozen US aircraft left RAF Mildenhall after a Wall Street Journal report of an Iranian threat.
